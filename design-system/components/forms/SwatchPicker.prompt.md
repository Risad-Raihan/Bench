One-line: pick a venture's identity colour at onboarding — the one choice that themes its header, card edge, dot, tabs and events.

```jsx
<SwatchPicker value={color} onChange={setColor} note="Themes the venture everywhere" />
```
Swatches preview the real header wash, not a flat chip. Twelve options ordered warm to cool — a venture never carries a colour outside that set, and two ventures sharing a colour is allowed.
